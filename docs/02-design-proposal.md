# Design proposal — v1

Status: **Implemented as reviewable direction, not owner-approved brand identity**.

## Positioning

**Good software starts with understanding.** DeduSoft helps organizations navigate from operational needs to practical software and digital experiences. Credibility comes from clear descriptions, real work and transparent product stages.

## Visual system

- **Direction:** Warm editorial technical studio, rather than a generic SaaS template.
- **Concept palette:** Warm ivory `#f7f6f2`, deep forest `#192a25`, soft leaf `#dce7d6`, lime accent `#cdf46b`. These are proposed website colors, not claimed official brand colors.
- **Typography:** Manrope and DM Sans with a restrained serif emphasis; system fallbacks preserve legibility if remote fonts cannot load.
- **Composition:** Wide typographic hero, diagrammatic delivery-process panel, four-service editorial grid, two product panels, text-led philosophy section, high-contrast contact band.
- **Interactions:** Visible keyboard focus, responsive navigation, subtle hover movement and reduced-motion support.
- **No generated fake product UI, stock testimonials, fabricated analytics or invented logos.** The website wordmark is a temporary typographic treatment.

## Content hierarchy / sitemap

1. **Home** — business positioning and primary CTA; services; DWIIS/Eductrack; working principles.
2. **Services** — four service pillars, deliverables and expected outcomes.
3. **Work** — only publishable, verified initiatives.
4. **Work / DWIIS** — public distribution evidence and status.
5. **Work / Eductrack** — development-stage explanation.
6. **Products** — clear product directory.
7. **About** — founder-led practice and values without fabricated team size.
8. **Insights** — editorial holding page rather than fabricated articles.
9. **Contact** — external public contact channel until a verified business email or form service is available.
10. **Privacy / Terms** — draft, pending legal and operational review.
11. **404** — graceful navigation recovery.

## Review items

Approve/revise palette, official logo, product wording, services actually sold, geographic market, public contact method, public portfolios, and legal text. The website must not be launched without owner review.
